About关于我

Hi, I'm Yihui.你好，我是艺卉。

I graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts and have several years of experience in design, including corporate branding, website design, and data product interfaces.我毕业于鲁迅美术学院，拥有多年的设计经验，曾参与企业品牌系统、网站设计以及数据产品界面等项目。

My practice moves between structured design work and independent visual storytelling.我的实践在系统化设计与个人视觉叙事之间展开。

Alongside commercial projects, I continue to develop personal work through illustration and publishing.在商业设计之外，我也持续进行插画与出版创作。

I enjoy design that balances clarity with playfulness.我喜欢在清晰结构与自由表达之间寻找平衡，让设计既精致又带有一点有趣的气质。
